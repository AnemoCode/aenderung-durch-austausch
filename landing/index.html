Simple setup

Running in under two minutes

3
simple steps
No LaTeX knowledge required on our end. Your files stay on Overleaf — we just read the metrics and show you the trends.
1
Connect Overleaf

Generate a Git token from your Overleaf account settings under Account → Git Integration. Paste it into Overlytics — it's stored encrypted on our end.

2
Add your projects

Paste your Overleaf project Git URLs. Overlytics clones and analyzes them automatically — detecting your main .tex file and running a full metrics scan.

3
Track your progress

Watch word count, pages, citations, and formulas grow over time on a 30-day chart. Projects sync in the background automatically — or trigger a manual sync anytime.
